# Sub 20 Challenge: coaching notes

These rules come from Erim's first two weeks of training (2–13 Nov 2026). They are read before every weekly plan, and the app also sends them to the coach.

## Recovery rules (after the 12 Nov session, where Erim started sore and ended exhausted)
1. **At least 72 h between hard sessions.** Hard sessions are intervals, goal pace, tempo, time trials and football. Leave at least two easy or rest days between them. Never schedule two hard sessions 48 h apart.
2. **At most 2 hard sessions a week, and football counts as one.** A match averaged 185 bpm for 55 min, which makes it a full hard session.
3. **The day after a hard session is rest or recovery:** at most 5 km, HR under 150.
4. **The day before a hard session is rest or easy:** at most 5 km.
5. **Weekly running km rises by 10% at most.** Week 2 jumped about 30% and contributed to the fatigue.
6. **Pace ranges are caps.** Tempo at 4:23 against a 4:28–4:34 target cost the next session. Running faster than the range is not a bonus.
7. **Check soreness before every hard session.** If legs or calves are sore after the warm-up, make it an easy run and move the session; never push through.

## Findings about Erim
- **Max HR is 210** (set by Erim; observed 203 running and 207 at football). Easy runs stay under about 158 bpm.
- **Easy runs with friends drift to Z3** (5:22/km at 175 bpm on 11 Nov). Plan social runs only on days where moderate effort is fine, or remind him to hold his own pace.
- **Strides belong at the end of easy runs.** Strides in the middle made the second half harder (Erim's note, 11 Nov).
- **Rep 1 tends to start too fast** (3:56 against a 4:06–4:10 target on 6 Nov). Cap the first rep at the target pace.
- **Jog the recoveries; walking left him stiff** (6 Nov).
- **He got a side stitch after eating** (6 Nov). Eat 3 h or more before quality sessions.
- **After a big dinner, swap in an easy day** (5 Nov). Swapping easy and hard days works well for him.
- **Form:** cadence stays flat (162–173 spm) as pace rises, so speed comes from longer steps and he overstrides at 5K pace. Height 172 cm. Aim for about 175–180 spm with about 1.40 m steps at 4:00/km.
- **Tempo felt great when fresh** (10 Nov: "never had any aching"). He responds well to threshold work.
- **Fitness on 13 Nov:** VDOT about 47–48 (tempo 4:23 at 88% of max HR; 800s at about 4:05). That predicts about 20:30–21:00. The 2 Nov baseline (22:04) was sub-maximal.

## Format preferences
- Only strides and walks are written in seconds. Reps, tempo blocks and jogs are written in distance.
- Planned km include warm-up, strides, recoveries and cool-down.
- Keep text short: no "why" explanations, 1–3 tips per run, and shoe names only.
- Run analysis covers only that run. The goal outlook belongs on the Progress page.
